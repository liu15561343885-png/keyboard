PrivateKeyboard bundled dataset 2026.11.1
=========================================

This directory contains project-built binary dictionaries, a language model, a
Core ML micro-model, and metadata. It is not raw third-party source data.

  https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
- Tatoeba Chinese sentences, CC BY 2.0 FR, https://tatoeba.org/
  (only aggregate bigram/trigram counts are used)
- jieba (Sun Junyi), MIT, https://github.com/fxsjy/jieba
